From Hide to Hold

Four steps. No shortcuts.

Every bag passes through the same four stages, in the same order, on the same bench — because that order is the only reason the stitching outlasts the leather.

  1. 01

    Source the Hide

    We choose full-grain hides for grain and character, not just size — every scar and wrinkle stays in the final bag.

  2. 02

    Cut & Skive

    Pattern pieces are cut by hand, and edges are skived thin so every fold sits flat instead of bulging.

  3. 03

    Saddle-Stitch

    Two needles, one waxed thread, pulled tight by hand — the same stitch used on horse tack for two centuries.

  4. 04

    Burnish & Finish

    Edges are burnished smooth, brass hardware is set, and every strap is load-tested before it leaves the bench.
